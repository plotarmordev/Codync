What changed

  • In the failed state the progress bar is gone and the error text takes its place on the same row.
  • Equal gaps left and right: the row's 10 px between the play glyph and the error, and 10 px from the error to the bubble's edge.
  • The same on iPhone, Mac and Android, in light and dark; a note that can't play keeps its length for the next try.
