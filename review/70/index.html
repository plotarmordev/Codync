A voice message on its own: Download only in the message's menu

Head df43b8dca (dev, 2.50.0) · Mac, iPhone (iOS Simulator) and Android at full size. Tap a shot to enlarge.

What changed

  • A sound sent on its own is the same player as a voice note inside a message: play, the bar and the clock, the file's name under the row — and no Download button on the row.
  • Download is in the message's menu, after Copy: right-click or the ⋯ button on the Mac, press and hold on the phones.
  • The one player for both kinds of sound landed in dev with the voice-note cards; the missing part was the phones' check, which now runs for a sound sent on its own (no Download button on the row, Download in the hold menu).

Why the released app still showed the button

A sound sent on its own was drawn by a second component, a file card with its own Download button, while a voice note inside a message already used the shared player with Download only in its menu. Dev routes both kinds through that one player; the released build predates it.
