Full-size design checklist (builder)

Checked each capture above at 1:1 on Mac, iPhone and Android, light and dark.

  • ✅ Hover/press chip radius matches its shape: a circle centered on the round play glyph (Mac hover and pressed, iPhone and Android pressed).
  • ✅ Even left/right padding: play→bar and bar→clock gaps equal (Mac 10/10 px measured; iPhone HStack spacing 12 pt on both sides; Android 32 px vs 35 px is the digit's built-in side bearing, the layout gap is Row spacedBy(12.dp) on both sides).
  • ✅ Nothing spills out: clock, bar and error text stay inside the bubble for 0:05, 4:30 and 1:02:00 notes; the chip never covers the bar or clock.
  • ✅ No sharp on/off: hover/press chip fades over the app's hover timing; pressed only deepens the same chip, no filled glyph.
  • ✅ Time shown before play: every rest capture shows 0:00 / length; the clock keeps the note's own form (m:ss or h:mm:ss) while playing, so the bar's end does not move.
  • ✅ Loading: spinner in the play slot, clock already in place, no layout jump to the playing frame.
  • ✅ Failed: error text takes the bar's place, in the danger color, inside the bubble, in both themes.
  • ✅ No size label or extra buttons; download lives in the message menu.
  • Note: iPhone captures are each taken after a fresh launch in that appearance. Switching appearance while a chat is open leaves its bubbles in the old colors (every bubble, not only voice notes); that is pre-existing in ConversationList.swift on dev, outside this change, and was what the old failed · light shot showed; ios/failed-light.png is now retaken on the current head in light mode and shows light bubbles.
